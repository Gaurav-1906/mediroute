About the lab

Explainable routing, not emergency dispatch.

MediRoute is a deterministic educational simulator. It uses a small in-memory road network and Dijkstra’s shortest-path algorithm to make every calculation inspectable for a DSA mini-project demonstration.

Backend-ownedRoutes, costs, path reconstruction, and trace steps are calculated by the API.
Simulated dataTravel times are fixed estimates. No live traffic, dispatch, or verified directions are provided.
